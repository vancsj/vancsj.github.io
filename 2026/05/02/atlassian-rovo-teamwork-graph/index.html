Atlassian Rovo 的底座：Teamwork Graph 与三层检索 / Inside Atlassian Rovo: The Teamwork Graph and a Three-Layer Search Stack

发表于 2026-05-02 更新于 2026-09-07 阅读次数：

Atlassian’s own State of Teams 2025 report puts a quarter of the workweek into looking for information. That is the number Rovo is aimed at, and it is the number I went looking for evidence behind — because most enterprise AI launches over the past two years have been a chat window bolted onto existing software, with no grasp of your company’s permissions or context.

Atlassian 自己的 State of Teams 2025 报告说，一周工作时间里有四分之一花在找信息上。Rovo 冲的就是这个数字。我去扒它的技术底座，是因为过去两年的企业 AI 发布基本都是一个套路：给现有软件加个聊天窗口，连你公司内部的权限和上下文都搞不清楚。

A side note: the other stat you often see next to it, “switching between 10+ SaaS tools a day”, is not Atlassian’s. It comes from Asana’s 2021 Anatomy of Work Index, and the two frequently get quoted as one finding.

旁注：另一个常被引用的「每天在 10 个以上 SaaS 工具之间切换」不是 Atlassian 的数据，出自 Asana 2021 年的 Anatomy of Work Index。两个数字经常被混在一起用。

It All Runs on the Teamwork Graph / 能力都来自 Teamwork Graph

All of Rovo’s capabilities are built on the Teamwork Graph, which Atlassian has barely promoted. It’s a unified data layer that takes every fragmented work element in your company — Jira tasks, Confluence docs, Slack messages, GitHub PRs — and turns them into standardized objects, then maps the relationships between them: who owns what, which decision links to which document.

Rovo 的所有能力都建立在 Teamwork Graph 上，Atlassian 一直没怎么宣传它。它的本质是一个统一数据层，把企业里所有零散的工作元素（Jira 任务、Confluence 文档、Slack 消息、GitHub PR）都抽象成标准对象，再把这些对象之间的关系映射出来：谁负责什么，哪个决策关联哪份文档。

Under the hood it’s graph theory: each object carries a set of attributes, and external data like Google Drive files and Slack messages goes through the same object model.

技术上用的是图论：每个对象带一组属性，Google Drive 文件、Slack 消息这类外部数据也走同一个对象模型。

Think of it this way: your company’s data used to sit as scattered files in separate rooms, and the Teamwork Graph stitches it into one map of the work. It syncs source-system permissions in real time, and the developer docs currently list 100 out-of-the-box connectors for external SaaS tools, not just Atlassian’s own products. That number was “50 and counting” at Team ‘25 in April 2025, so it has doubled in about a year.

打个比方：以前你的公司数据像散落在各个房间的文件，Teamwork Graph 把它们拼成了一张完整的工作地图。它实时同步源系统的权限，开发者文档目前列出 100 个预构建连接器，除了 Atlassian 自己的产品，外部 SaaS 的数据也能整合进来。这个数字在 2025 年 4 月的 Team ‘25 上是「50 个，还在增加」，一年左右翻了一倍。

Atlassian’s documentation defines four relationship types: canonical (direct structural connections), activity (user interaction behaviors), logical (high-level abstractions), and inferred (implicit connections derived by machine learning). Combine those and Rovo can reconstruct how a decision was reached.

Atlassian 官方文档定义了四种关系类型：规范关系（直接的结构连接）、活动关系（用户交互行为）、逻辑关系（高层抽象关系）、推断关系（通过机器学习推导出的隐含连接）。这些关系组合起来，Rovo 就能还原一个决策当初是怎么形成的。

Search Architecture: BM25 + kNN + Reranking / 搜索架构：BM25 + kNN + 重排序

Rovo’s semantic search runs a hybrid retrieval architecture, not just vector similarity. According to Atlassian’s engineering blog, the search stack has three layers:

Rovo 的语义搜索跑的是一套混合检索架构，不只做向量相似度计算。根据 Atlassian 工程团队的博客，搜索栈分为三层：

  1. L1 retrieval: built on AWS OpenSearch, combining BM25 keyword matching and kNN semantic retrieval, with gradient-boosted-decision-tree reranking over popularity and contributor features.
    L1 检索层：基于 AWS OpenSearch，结合 BM25 关键词匹配和 kNN 语义检索，再用梯度提升决策树模型按热门度、贡献者等特征重排序。
  2. L2 semantic and behavioral ranking: fine-tuned cross-encoders plus a Deep & Cross Network, using behavioral signals such as clicking a result or time spent on a page.
    L2 语义与行为排序层：精调的跨编码器加 DCN（Deep & Cross Network），结合用户行为信号（点了哪个结果、在页面上停留多久）做二次排序。
  3. L3 cross-product interleaving: a final interleave of results from Jira, Confluence and third-party tools, weighted by product affinity and query-intent classification.
    L3 跨产品交织层：对 Jira、Confluence、第三方工具的结果做最终交织，按产品亲和度和查询意图分类调整权重。

Rovo has been through three embedding models: MiniLM for early prototyping, then BGE-large for work-shaped language like Jira issues, now EmbeddingGemma-300m, which balances quality, latency and cost.

Rovo 的嵌入模型换过三代：早期用 MiniLM 做快速原型，后来换成 BGE-large 处理 Jira 工单这类工作语言，现在是 EmbeddingGemma-300m，在质量、延迟和成本之间取平衡。

In March 2026 Atlassian worked with NVIDIA to fine-tune Llama-Nemotron-Embed-1B-V2 using the NeMo toolchain, reporting a 26%-40% improvement in retrieval quality on Recall@60 and NDCG@1 against the un-fine-tuned base model. That is Atlassian’s own measurement on its own corpus, not a third-party benchmark.

2026 年 3 月，Atlassian 和 NVIDIA 合作，用 NeMo 工具链微调了 Llama-Nemotron-Embed-1B-V2，报告的检索质量提升是 26%-40%（用 Recall@60 和 NDCG@1 衡量，基线是未微调的原模型）。这是 Atlassian 在自己语料上的自测数字，不是第三方基准。

Smart Search: Retrieval by Intent / 智能搜索：按意图检索

Rovo Search’s differentiation is semantic understanding. Search “Why did we delay the Q3 launch?” and it looks for issues, pages and discussions that explain causes and decisions — even ones that never literally say “delay” or “Q3”.

Rovo Search 的差异化在于语义理解。你搜「为什么 Q3 发布延期了？」，它会去找解释原因和决策的问题、页面和讨论，即使这些内容里从未出现过「延期」或「Q3」字样。

Knowledge Cards are the more useful part day to day: search a project and get owners, current progress and related docs in one card. It also auto-defines company-specific jargon, so new hires stop having to ask what each acronym means.

更实用的是知识卡片：搜一个项目，负责人、当前进度、相关文档直接弹出来。还有自动定义功能，新员工不用再挨个问某个缩写是什么意思。

Search also shifts result priority by context: issues and epics that unblock execution in Jira, specs and decisions and runbooks in Confluence, and related work chained together across tools — incident to fix to follow-up.

搜索还会根据上下文调整结果优先级：在 Jira 里优先给能推进执行的问题和史诗，在 Confluence 里优先给规范、决策和运行手册，跨工具搜索时把相关工作串起来（事件、修复、后续跟进）。

Context-Aware Chat: Three Reasoning Modes / 上下文感知聊天：三种推理模式

Rovo Chat sits in the Jira and Confluence sidebars. It reads your current work context — edit a PRD and invoke it, and it knows the page content and can pull in related Jira task data.

Rovo Chat 就嵌在 Jira 和 Confluence 的侧边栏里。它能读到当前的工作上下文：你正在编辑产品需求文档时调用它，它知道当前页面的内容，能直接补充相关的 Jira 任务数据。

Between 2025 and 2026 Rovo Chat added three reasoning modes. The response times below are Atlassian’s own published figures.

2025 到 2026 年，Rovo Chat 增加了三种推理模式。下面的响应时间是 Atlassian 官方公布的数字。

Mode 模式 Response time 响应时间 Reasoning depth 推理深度 Use case 适用场景
Standard chat 标准对话 5–10 sec Basic text processing 基础文本处理 Q&A, summaries 简单问答、摘要生成
Think Deeper 1–3 min Structured decomposition and verification 结构化任务拆解与验证 Financial risk analysis, proposal comparison 财务风险分析、技术提案对比
Deep Research 10–15 min Cross-domain synthesis 全域数据综合研究 Feedback trend synthesis, competitor analysis 客户反馈趋势合成、竞争对手分析

Deep Research runs a multi-agent framework. Atlassian’s engineering write-up describes planners, reflectors and a proofreader agent, and a mixed model stack rather than a single vendor: Llama 3.2 8B (Atlassian-hosted) for query understanding, GPT-4o and GPT-4.1 for sub-agent tasks, and Claude 3.7 Sonnet’s thinking mode for planning and reflection. That post is from August 2025, so the specific model choices may well have moved since.

Deep Research 用的是多智能体框架。Atlassian 的工程博客写的是 planner、reflector 和一个 proofreader 代理，而且模型栈是混搭的，不是单一厂商：查询理解用 Atlassian 自托管的 Llama 3.2 8B，子代理任务用 GPT-4o 和 GPT-4.1，规划和反思用 Claude 3.7 Sonnet 的思考模式。那篇博客是 2025 年 8 月发的，具体的模型选择现在很可能已经换过。

The v2 release in December 2025 added a Self-Evolution Engine — it evolves queries as discoveries come in and decides on its own when the research is complete — and a Memory Bank, which builds a per-section, per-claim record of sources so every claim links back. Atlassian’s stated v1 problems were shallow reports and a fire-and-forget workflow you couldn’t steer mid-run, not missing citations; v1 already shipped cited reports.

2025 年 12 月的 v2 加了两样东西：自我进化引擎，根据已有发现动态调整查询，自己判断研究什么时候算完；以及 Memory Bank，按章节和声明建立来源记录，让每个声明都能链回出处。Atlassian 说的 v1 问题是报告太浅、流程一次性跑完没法中途干预，不是没有引用，v1 就已经带引用了。

Agents: From Tools to Team Members / 智能代理：从工具到团队成员

Rovo Agents are configurable AI teammates that any team member can invoke or create. Out-of-the-box ones include writing assistants, decision support and customer-insight analysis. Custom agents get built in the Studio app, launched at Team ‘25 in April 2025, with or without code.

Rovo Agents 是可配置的 AI 团队成员，任何团队成员都可以调用或创建。开箱即用的代理包括写作助手、决策辅助、客户洞察分析等。自定义代理在 Studio 应用里建，2025 年 4 月的 Team ‘25 上发布，写不写代码都行。

Dev Agents launched in 2025 for developers, covering the path from business requirement to implementation. The Code Reviewer Agent analyses pull requests against the technical requirements and acceptance criteria in Jira. The beta programme ended on 26 January 2026 and these agents are now generally available inside the unified Rovo Dev.

Dev Agents 在 2025 年发布，面向开发者，覆盖从业务需求到技术实现的整条链路。代码审查代理会对照 Jira 中的技术需求和验收标准分析拉取请求。Beta 项目已于 2026 年 1 月 26 日结束，这些代理现在并入正式发布的 Rovo Dev。

Agents can be invoked from Chat, from automation rules, from the Confluence and Jira editors via /Rovo or /ai, and from Studio. Atlassian said nearly 2,000 Rovo Agents had been integrated into customer workflows — that figure is from April 2025 and has not been restated since; Team ‘26 reports different metrics instead, such as 14 million Rovo-assisted actions a month.

代理可以在多个地方调用：Rovo Chat、自动化规则、Confluence 和 Jira 的编辑界面（用 /Rovo 或 /ai）、以及 Studio。Atlassian 说已有近 2000 个 Rovo 代理集成到客户工作流中，这个数字来自 2025 年 4 月，之后没再更新；Team ‘26 换了口径，报的是每月 1400 万次 Rovo 辅助操作。

Rovo Everywhere: Canvas and Personal Memory / Rovo Everywhere：Canvas 与个人记忆

In October 2025 Atlassian launched “Rovo Everywhere”, extending the platform to Canvas, Personal Memory (preferences and context remembered across sessions) and expanded governance controls.

2025 年 10 月，Atlassian 推出了 Rovo Everywhere，把平台扩展到 Canvas、个人记忆（跨会话记住用户偏好和上下文）和更强的治理功能。

Canvas is a collaboration surface where you write content together with Rovo. Its backend is a top-level orchestrator agent with a set of specialised skills: cross-product search including third-party products, Teamwork Graph search, a Jira retrieval agent.

Canvas 是一个协作界面，用户可以和 Rovo 一起写内容。它的后端围绕一个顶级编排代理构建，这个代理能调用一组专门的技能：跨产品搜索（包括第三方产品）、Teamwork Graph 搜索、Jira 检索代理。

For content generation the LLM produces ADF — Atlassian Document Format, a nested JSON structure — directly, then passes it through a proprietary ADF repair library to guarantee validity. Whiteboard streaming uses a streaming SVG parser alongside constraint-solving algorithms, so you watch the board assemble live.

内容生成上，LLM 直接产出 ADF（Atlassian 文档格式，一种嵌套 JSON 结构），再通过专有的 ADF 修复库保证有效性。白板的流式传输用流式 SVG 解析器配合约束求解算法，所以你能实时看着板子被拼出来。

What the Marketing Leaves Out / 官方材料里没写的部分

The architecture above is genuinely well-built. The parts Atlassian’s own launch posts skip are worth just as much attention.

上面这套架构确实做得扎实。Atlassian 自己的发布文章跳过的部分，同样值得看。

Consumption billing. Rovo now comes bundled into paid plans, but usage draws on a pooled monthly credit allowance — around 25 credits per user on Standard, 70 on Premium, 150 on Enterprise — and a single Deep Research request costs roughly 100 credits. So one report can consume several users’ monthly allowance. That turns a predictable per-seat cost into a variable one, and the numbers here come from third-party pricing analysis rather than a single canonical Atlassian page, so treat them as the shape of the model rather than exact figures.

按用量计费。 Rovo 现在捆进付费方案里，但用量走的是每月的 credits 池：Standard 每用户约 25、Premium 70、Enterprise 150，而一次 Deep Research 请求大约要 100 credits。也就是说一份报告能吃掉好几个用户的月度额度。这把可预测的按席位成本变成了浮动成本。这几个数字来自第三方定价分析，不是 Atlassian 某一个权威页面，所以当作计费模型的形状看，别当精确值。

Hallucination shows up in the boring places. One Atlassian Community thread describes the Rovo OKR Generator inventing key results twice inside 45 minutes. Another describes Rovo confidently producing entire fake releases and sprints when the underlying data didn’t exist. These are individual customer reports, not a measured error rate, and Atlassian has published no hallucination benchmark for Rovo.

幻觉出现在最平常的地方。 Atlassian 社区有个帖子说 Rovo 的 OKR 生成器在 45 分钟内两次编出不存在的关键结果。另一个帖子说底层数据不存在时，Rovo 会一脸自信地造出整个不存在的发布和迭代。这些是单个客户的报告，不是可测的错误率，Atlassian 也没有公布过 Rovo 的幻觉基准。

Permissions and connectors leak into answer quality. Atlassian’s own troubleshooting guidance concedes that Rovo “may generate incomplete or outdated answers when source content is unavailable because of permissions or connector limitations”. The failure mode is quiet: you get an answer, just not one built on everything relevant.

权限和连接器会影响答案质量。 Atlassian 自己的排查文档承认，当源内容因权限或连接器限制而不可用时，Rovo「可能生成不完整或过时的答案」。这个失败模式很安静：你会拿到一个答案，只是它没建立在所有相关内容上。

Rollout control is limited. Multiple customers report that once Rovo is on a site it can be restricted but not fully removed, and one early rollout had to be rolled back.

上线控制有限。 多个客户报告说 Rovo 一旦在站点上启用，只能限制不能完全移除，而且有一次提前上线被回滚了。

No neutral comparison exists. Every Rovo-versus-Glean or Rovo-versus-Microsoft-365-Copilot comparison I could find is written by one of the vendors or by consultancies doing SEO. There is no controlled third-party test. That is not a verdict on which product retrieves better — it means the question is currently unanswerable from public evidence.

没有中立的对比。 我能找到的所有 Rovo 对 Glean、Rovo 对 Microsoft 365 Copilot 的对比，都出自厂商自己或者做 SEO 的咨询公司，没有受控的第三方测试。这不是在说哪个产品检索得更好，而是说这个问题目前从公开证据里得不出答案。

It is Atlassian-centric by design. The value comes from Jira and Confluence being the system of record. For an organization not centred on them, most of the graph is empty.

它天生以 Atlassian 为中心。 价值来自 Jira 和 Confluence 就是记录系统这个前提。对一个不围着它们转的组织，这张图大部分是空的。

From System of Record to System of Action / 从记录系统到行动系统

Legacy enterprise collaboration software is mostly a system of record: it stores data, tracks processes, leaves an audit trail. Rovo aims at a system of action — surfacing context, aiding decisions, automating repetitive work.

以前的企业协作软件主要是记录系统：存数据、记流程、留痕迹。Rovo 想做的是行动系统：主动推上下文、帮着做决策、自动化重复工作。

You used to have to ask before it answered. Now it reads the Teamwork Graph and pushes relevant information to you. It carries goals, a role and knowledge, so it works more like a team member than a tool. A hundred pre-built connectors and one unified data layer wire together products that never talked to each other.

以前你得先问，它才答。现在它基于 Teamwork Graph 主动把相关信息推给你。它带着目标、角色和知识，用起来更像一个团队成员而不是一个工具。100 个预构建连接器加上统一数据层，把原本互不相通的产品接到了一起。

Atlassian said Rovo passed 5 million monthly active users in its Q2 FY26 shareholder letter, dated February 2026, and has not published a specific MAU figure since — later letters describe growth qualitatively instead. So the honest summary is that the plumbing is real and well-documented, and the outcome data is still mostly Atlassian’s own.

Atlassian 在 2026 年 2 月的 Q2 FY26 股东信里说 Rovo 月活跃用户突破 500 万，之后没再给过具体 MAU 数字，后面几封信只做定性描述。所以诚实的结论是：管道是真的，文档也齐，但效果数据到现在基本还是 Atlassian 自己给的。
